About

I am an assistant professor of mechanical engineering at IIT Gandhinagar. My research interests broadly lie in continuum mechanics and applied mathematics.

In particular, I am interested in the mechanics of thin bodies — such as strings, rods, ribbons, membranes, and plates — and their applications to engineering problems.
